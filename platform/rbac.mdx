title: RBAC
description: Viewer, operator, and administrator authorization
---

Pravidhi uses role-based access control.

| Role | Typical capabilities |
| --- | --- |
| viewer | health, status, read-only information |
| operator | approved execution and operational actions |
| admin | administrative configuration and privileged operations |

The exact endpoint policy is deployment-specific and should be reviewed before production exposure.

## Tenant boundary

A valid identity without a tenant is not sufficient for tenant-scoped operations.

Every filesystem and execution operation should resolve inside the authenticated tenant context.

## Least privilege

Grant operator/admin only where necessary. Do not make OAuth users administrators by default.

## Approval boundary

RBAC answers **who may request** an action. Approval answers **whether this sensitive action may proceed now**. These are separate controls.
