## Core principles

- **Explicit authorization** — machine operations require an authenticated identity and role.
- **Tenant isolation** — workspace paths are scoped to a tenant.
- **Approval-aware execution** — sensitive operations can require explicit approval.
- **Least privilege** — the agent should expose only the capabilities required by the integration.
- **Auditability** — security-relevant actions produce structured audit events.
- **No secrets in source** — OAuth secrets, signing keys and bearer tokens belong in secret storage or environment configuration.
